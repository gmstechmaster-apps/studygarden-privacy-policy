Aurdio Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Your music, your listening activity and Aurdio's AI features stay on your device.

This policy explains how Aurdio, a local music player for Android published by AppCraftTechnologies ("we", "us"), handles your information. Aurdio is designed to work entirely on your device: we do not run servers that receive your data, and we do not collect personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal information. Aurdio does not require an account, sign-in or email address.

  • Aurdio does not upload your music files or your library information anywhere.
  • Aurdio does not send what you type into the AI assistant ("Ask Aurdio") to any server. AI processing runs entirely on your device and never uses a cloud AI service.
  • Aurdio does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location, and never asks for permission to do so.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

To work as a music player, Aurdio stores the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Music library information — song, album and artist details read from the music folders you choose, and references to those files. Aurdio only accesses the folders you select using Android's own folder picker; it does not request broad access to your storage.
  • Playback state and preferences — your queue, play history, favorites, playlists, EQ and sound settings, output settings and appearance choices.
  • EQ and equipment profiles — including, if you use the AI sound assistant, the request you typed (for example, "make the bass hit harder") and which on-device model produced the result, so you can see how a sound profile was created.
  • The optional on-device AI model, if you choose to download it (see section 3).

Information about connected audio devices (such as a USB DAC's supported formats) is read locally to configure playback and is not sent anywhere.

3. Network Use

Every core feature of Aurdio works fully offline. The app makes one kind of network connection, and only when you ask it to: if you choose to download the optional local AI model from Settings → Downloads / Local Model, Aurdio downloads the model file (Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, Apache License 2.0) directly from Hugging Face, the model's official host.

This is a plain file download. It does not include your name, your music library, your AI requests or any other personal information. As with any internet connection, Hugging Face can see your device's IP address while serving the file; its handling of that information is governed by Hugging Face's privacy policy.

4. Analytics, Crash Reporting and Advertising

Aurdio does not contain any analytics, crash-reporting, tracking or advertising SDKs. No usage data is collected or sent to us or to third parties, and Aurdio shows no ads.

5. Backups

Aurdio does not use Android's automatic cloud backup. If you choose to create a backup of your settings and profiles (Settings → Backup & Restore), Aurdio writes the backup file to a location you pick using Android's file picker. If you choose a folder that syncs to a cloud service you use, that file is then handled by that service under its own terms. We never receive your backup files.

6. Permissions

  • Notifications and foreground service (media playback) — to show playback controls and keep music playing while the app is in the background.
  • Internet — used only for the optional AI model download described in section 3.

7. Purchases

Aurdio is a paid app sold through Google Play. Your purchase and payment are processed entirely by Google Play under Google's own terms and privacy policy; we never receive or store your payment details. Aurdio has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. If that changes, this policy will be updated first to describe exactly what is processed and by whom. See also our Terms of Service.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data is stored only on your device, you are in full control of it:

  • Delete all locally stored library data at any time from Settings → Privacy → "Delete all local library data".
  • Delete the downloaded AI model at any time from Settings → Downloads / Local Model.
  • Uninstalling Aurdio removes everything the app has stored on your device. Backup files you exported yourself remain wherever you saved them until you delete them.

9. Security

Aurdio's data is kept in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. A downloaded AI model is integrity-checked against its published checksum before it can be used.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in the European Economic Area under the GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Since we do not collect or hold any personal data about you, there is nothing on our side to access or delete — all of your data is on your device and under your control as described above. You are welcome to contact us with any privacy question.

11. Children's Privacy

Aurdio is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children, and the app does not collect personal data from any user.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy, for example if a future version adds a new feature that handles data differently. Any change will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date, and described plainly. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
